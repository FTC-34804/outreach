### innovation Project

- this is by far the most rewarding part of the FLL
- heres how to succeed 

---

### step 1: get a problem

- first find a problem that you want to solve that is related to biodiversity.
- research the problem, talk to experts, gain a deep understanding of your problem

---

### step 2: design a solution(s)

- start researching and build a basic version of your solution, or if you have too many ideas and existential indecisiveness make a basic version of them
- decide if it'll be a hardware or software project (or both), and pick the appropriate version control/collaborative workspace. (we recommend github)

---

### step 3: create or release an implementation of your project

- if its a software project, opensource it, and if its a hardware project, build a prototype.
- go around and use your skills as annoying pricks to advertise and beg for feedback. :D
- get it reviewed by your peers, maybe your parents (but dont be too annoying to them), and if you can, experts.
### IMPORTANT: DO NOT vibecode your project, you'll look like an idiot when presenting.

---

### step 4: improve your solution

- use your feedback and testing data to improve your solution. Consider adding new features and stuffs :)
- try and get your project field tested or used by a larger audience and/or experts and try to create real impact. This is the hardest step. You can't be embarrassed to do stuff, you just gotta do it.

---

### step 5: publicise

- try and maximise the impact of your project as much as possible. Keep getting feedback and repeatedly improve your project.

---

### documentation

- document your progress in robot game AND your progress in your innovation project, you can date it and keep track of your progress. this way you dont have to waste 40 minutes of the next session thinking about what you did last session.
- also, you will need this when deciding what parts to put in your final presentation.
